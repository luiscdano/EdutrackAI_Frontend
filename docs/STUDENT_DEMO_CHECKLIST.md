# EduTrack student demo checklist

Use this checklist before the final presentation. It focuses on the student journey rather than isolated screens.

1. Sign in as a student and open onboarding.
2. Choose an institution and academic program.
3. Pick a reference period, remove subjects that are not being taken, search for a subject from another period, and verify both selections remain active.
4. Confirm the final mixed-period subject list and save.
5. On Home, verify the primary action is compact enough that `Pregúntale a EduTrack` is visible without excessive scrolling.
6. Collapse and expand the desktop sidebar; reload and verify the preference persists.
7. In Materias, verify long titles do not move the difficulty selector or action buttons. Change difficulty and confirm the page does not flash or jump to the top.
8. Open Practicar with a student that has no generated plan/quizzes. It must still show active subjects with a useful `Repasar` action.
9. Open Recursos for a subject. Search using a real topic such as `MAUI`; generic words such as `video` must not be treated as academic topics.
10. Ask EduTrack a question that mentions a subject but not a concrete topic. It should ask for the topic instead of sending the whole phrase to external search.
11. Add a deadline and return Home. The deadline should influence `Lo próximo` and the recommended action.
12. Add a grade and verify Progreso stays student-friendly (no internal risk score/component math).
13. Add a professor/class material URL and verify it appears ahead of generic external sources for that subject/topic.
14. Complete a Focus/practice flow and confirm progress/session data updates without breaking navigation.

## Demo rule

If a signal is missing, EduTrack should say what it still needs and offer a next action. No primary student screen should be empty or pretend certainty it does not have.
